Keep your farm safe and play it on another device

Open Settings in the game and tap Link Apple/Google. On a new iPhone or iPad, install Barnbury and sign in with the same Apple or Google account: your town comes back. A town linked with Google can also be opened in the Android version.

Purchases

  • Items you buy are added to your town at once. If something did not arrive, restart the game while online; the App Store hands over any purchase that is still waiting.
  • Farm Pass: in the Farm Pass window, tap Restore Purchases to bring an active subscription to this device.
  • Cancel the Farm Pass at any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from the game's Farm Pass window (Manage). It stays active until the end of the period already paid.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

In the game: Settings → Delete account, then hold the red button. Your town, its online copy, profile, friends, club membership and messages are deleted for good. If you no longer have the game, e-mail us from the address above with your farm name and we will delete the account for you.

Notifications, sound and language

All are in the game's Settings. Reminders can also be turned off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Barnbury.

Report a player

If a farm or club name is offensive, e-mail us the name (and the invite code if you have it). We review every report and remove offending names and accounts.
