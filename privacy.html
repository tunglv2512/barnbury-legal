Barnbury: Farm & Town — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · LEAFMIND TECH LIMITED

This policy explains what information the game Barnbury: Farm & Town ("Barnbury", "the game") collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can control or delete it. Barnbury is made by LEAFMIND TECH LIMITED ("we", "us"), which is responsible for this information. Questions: levantung.leafmindtech@gmail.com.

Barnbury is a farming and town-building game for iPhone, iPad and Android. You can play without creating an account with us: the game gives every player an anonymous game account, and you can choose to link it to your Apple or Google account so your town can be restored on another device.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Farm and club names you type, and the portrait you pick from the game's avatars. Other players can see them (friends lists, visits, clubs, the market). A photo you choose as your avatar stays on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Apple or Google sign-in, only if you link your game account in Settings: the account's identifier and e-mail address (with Sign in with Apple you may share a private relay address instead) and, with Google, the name on the account. We use them only to sign you in and to show which account your town is linked to.
  • Messages to other players: club chat and notes in Barnbury use phrases chosen from a fixed list; we keep the ones you send so the recipients can read them.
  • Messages to support: if you write to us, the e-mail and what you send in it.

Information collected automatically

  • Game account and progress: an anonymous account ID, your town (the save file), level, experience, items, friends, likes, visits, gifts, club membership, market listings and invite codes, so the game works online and your town is kept safe.
  • Purchases: which in-app items you bought and the App Store transaction ID, so each purchase is delivered exactly once and kept with your account. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment card or Apple ID password.
  • Game use (analytics): how the game is played (for example levels reached, buildings placed, screens opened, ads watched, purchases made), the device model, operating system, language, country or region (derived from the device settings and IP address), and an app-instance identifier. We use this to understand and improve the game.
  • Crash and performance data: crash reports, device state at the time of a crash, frame rate and memory use, so we can fix problems.
  • App integrity: a token from Apple's App Attest / DeviceCheck that proves requests come from the genuine game, to protect players from cheating and abuse.
  • Advertising: the game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect the device's IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and, only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt, the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), to show, limit, measure and personalise ads. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised using the IDFA.

Barnbury does not collect your contacts, precise location, health data or browsing history. Reminders are local notifications planned on your device; no notification token is sent to us.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game: save and restore your town, sign you in, deliver purchases, and let you play with friends and clubs (the basis is our contract with you to provide the game).
  • To keep the game fair and secure: detect cheating, fraud and abuse (our legitimate interest).
  • To understand and improve the game and fix crashes (our legitimate interest).
  • To show ads that keep the game free, and, where you consent, personalised ads (your consent where the law requires it; you can change it at any time, see section 5).
  • To answer you when you contact support.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose not described here.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of the game for us, each bound by its own privacy commitments, which provide protection at least equal to this policy:

  • Google LLC — Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check), Google Sign-In, AdMob and the User Messaging Platform for consent. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps.
  • Apple Inc. — Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and App Attest. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Other players see your farm name, avatar, level, town and what you choose to share in the game (likes, gifts, club activity, market listings).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of players and of the game.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your game account, town, friends, club and purchase records: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account they are deleted from our servers.
  • Analytics event data: no longer than 14 months. Crash reports: about 90 days.
  • Support e-mails: as long as needed to answer you.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your town, its online copy, your profile, friends, club membership and messages; for a town linked with Sign in with Apple, the game also asks Apple to revoke its access to that Apple account. You can also ask us by e-mail.
  • Tracking: allow or deny it in Apple's prompt, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws): change your choices in the game at Settings → Privacy.
  • Notifications and photos: turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, write to levantung.leafmindtech@gmail.com with your farm name and your invite code (shown in the game's Friends window), so we can find your account. We will not treat you differently for using them.

6. Children

Barnbury is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and kept with Google Firebase, with access limited to what each part of the game needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The new version is posted on this page, and the effective date at the top shows when it took effect.
